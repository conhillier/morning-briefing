For CFOs - UK regulatory updates

HMRC late payment interest rate rises from 24 November 2026

The Bank of England base rate increased to 4.00% from 3.75% on 5 November 2026, triggering a rise in HMRC interest rates. Late payment interest will increase to base rate plus 4%; repayment interest will increase to base rate minus 1% with a minimum floor of 0.5%. Changes take effect on 16 November 2026 for quarterly instalment payments and 24 November 2026 for non-quarterly instalment payments.

Source: HMRC

Companies House pauses profit and loss filing; mandates iXBRL from April 2028

The government has decided to pause implementing the requirement for small companies and micro-entities to file profit and loss accounts with Companies House from April 2028, following recommendations of the Small Business Regulatory Taskforce. Companies will continue to be allowed to file abridged accounts and retain the option to 'fillet' profit and loss information after 1 April 2028. From April 2028, all UK registered companies must file accounts in Inline eXtensible Business Reporting Language (iXBRL) format using commercial software, with web and paper-based filing systems closed for accounts filings from that date.

Source: Companies House
